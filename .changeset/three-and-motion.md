`<Three>` and `<Motion>`: a Layer can hold a three.js scene (through React Three Fiber, or plain three.js in `setup`)
or a Motion sequence, both on the video's clock, so they scrub both ways and export as previewed. The new
`@cutcraft/libraries` package offers them to a cut file's free code.
