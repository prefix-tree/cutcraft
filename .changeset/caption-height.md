Captions can sit at any height. `style={{ position: 0.6 }}` on `<Captions>` puts their middle 60% of the way down the frame; `top`, `center` and `bottom` work as before. Over a talking head in a vertical video, `center` lands on the speaker's mouth and `bottom` is under the platforms' controls; a number between puts the captions under the chin.
