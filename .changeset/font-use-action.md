`font/use`, the action a font picker calls: it sets a family from the font catalogue on a title, the captions or a theme role. A Google family the file does not declare is loaded for the preview at once, and the fold writes its `googleFont()` declaration, adds the import and names it at the use site; a new weight of a declared family is added to its `weights`.
