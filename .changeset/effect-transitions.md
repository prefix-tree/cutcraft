Six built-in transitions drawn as shaders: `iris` (circle, polygon or star), `clockWipe`, `blinds`, `filmBurn`, `pixelDissolve` and `zoom`. They crossfade where there is no WebGL and fade at an overlay's edge. These names are now built in, so a file that declares its own transition under one of them needs to rename it.
