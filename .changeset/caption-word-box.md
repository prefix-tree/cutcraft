Captions can box the word being said. `style={{ highlightBox: "#4aa3ff" }}` on `<Captions>` puts a filled, rounded box behind the current word with any word animation (`word-highlight`, `karaoke`, `word-by-word`); `highlight` stays the colour of its letters. The box takes no room of its own, so the line does not move as the box passes along it.
