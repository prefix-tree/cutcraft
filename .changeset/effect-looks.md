Five new effects. `halftone` prints the picture as a dot screen, in one ink or in CMYK. `pixelate` makes a mosaic of the frame or of a box in it. `scanlines` adds the rolling lines of an old screen. `lightLeak` washes wandering warm light over the picture. `fractalNoise` blends evolving clouds of noise over it, as mist, texture or a moving background. They are shader effects with no CSS fallback, and every param keyframes. These names are now built in, so a file that declares its own effect under one of them needs to rename it.
