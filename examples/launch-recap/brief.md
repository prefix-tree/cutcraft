# Brief

Make a 25–30 second launch recap for **Shed**, a cloud for small software.

Open on the founder's key sentence from the interview. Cut to product b-roll
with a slow push-in, then the screen demo, then back to the founder for the
close. Put the product name and tagline on a title card at the top, a lower
third when the founder comes back, captions for everything the founder says,
and a music bed that ducks under speech. End on a card with the call to
action.

The product name, the tagline, the accent colour and the call to action must
be tweakable afterwards without touching the edit.

Assets are in `manifest.json`: the founder interview (with transcript, shots
and silences), product b-roll, a screen recording, a music track and the logo.
